Onwun is a two-person design studio. Based between Manchester and London, we build identities, websites and digital experiences for businesses who want to lead, not follow.

Our values:

01

Outcomes first, taste second

Every creative decision earns its place by doing useful work for the people and business it serves.

02

All in or nothing

We take on fewer projects so every partnership gets our full attention, judgement and care.

03

Human-first, always

Behind every brand is a real story and real problems. We never lose sight of who the work is for.

04

Considered, never rushed

We move at the pace the work actually needs: fast when it should be, patient when it matters more.

Why choose us:

  • You work directly with the two people actually designing your brand, from the first call to final delivery.
  • We take on fewer clients by design, so every project gets the depth of research and craft we’d want for our own studio.

Services:

  • Brand strategy 01 / 06

    Vision & mission

    Your vision is the future your brand is working towards; your mission is how it gets there, every day. Together they give the whole business a clear direction and a reason to believe in it.

    Positioning

    Your positioning is the space your brand owns in its market: who it’s for, what it believes and why it’s different. We map you against your competitors to find where you can lead, not follow.

    Values

    Your values are the handful of principles your brand won’t compromise on. We define what they are and what they mean in practice, so they guide how you work, hire and make decisions, not just sit on a wall.

    Tone of voice

    Your tone of voice is how your brand sounds, wherever it speaks. We define its personality in a few clear words and show how it reads in practice, so every message feels like it comes from the same place.

    Competitor research

    We study the brands you’re up against: how they look, what they say, how they work and where they make their money. Then we pinpoint the gaps and opportunities, so your brand stands apart rather than blending in.

    Audience persona

    We build detailed profiles of the people you want to reach: who they are, what they want and what’s holding them back. Knowing your audience this well shapes every decision, from the strategy to the smallest detail.
